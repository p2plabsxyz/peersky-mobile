# PeerSky Terms of Use

Last updated: October 1, 2026

PeerSky is a free, open source browser from P2P Labs, with PeerChat, P2PMD and
PeerTunes built in. By using it you agree to these terms. They are short on
purpose.

## PeerChat rules

PeerChat connects you straight to other people. There is no tolerance for
objectionable content or abusive users. Do not send or post:

- Sexual content involving anyone under 18. We report it to the authorities.
- Threats, harassment, bullying, or hate aimed at who someone is.
- Nudity or sexual content, in any room.
- Violence meant to shock, or anything that encourages self harm.
- Spam, scams, malware, or phishing links.
- Someone else's private information, or messages pretending to be someone else.
- Anything else that is illegal where you or the people you talk to live.

You need to be at least 16 to use PeerChat.

## When someone breaks them

- PeerChat filters messages for abuse, slurs and adult links, and refuses nude
  pictures before they are sent and again when they arrive.
- You can block anyone. Everything they send disappears for you, in every chat,
  and they can no longer message you directly.
- You can report anyone, or a single message, from inside the app. Reports go to
  contact@p2plabs.xyz and we read every one within 24 hours.
- In P2P Republic, the public room we run, we remove anyone who breaks these
  rules. Whoever creates any other room can remove people from it too.
- PeerChat has no server, so nobody, us included, can delete a message from a
  device it already reached.

## What you share

What you send in PeerChat, write in P2PMD, or publish to a drive is yours, and
you are responsible for it. It lives on your devices and on the devices of the
people you share it with. Anything published to a public `hyper://` address can
be opened by anyone who has the address, so publish only what you have the right
to share.
